## 🏆 New core papers

### IMLE-VLA: Fast Single-Step Action Generation for Vision-Language-Action Policies · **8.31**
IMLE-VLA replaces the iterative multi-step action head in vision-language-action policies with a single-step conditional generator trained via conditional Implicit Maximum Likelihood Estimation, achieving faster inference, higher success rates, and smoother robot motion.

_spd 9 · gen 7 · spec 8 · cost 8_ · [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10915) · [pdf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.10915v1)

### Memory as Plans: World-Action Modeling with Memory-Grounded Planning · **7.28**
MaP-WAM achieves state-of-the-art long-horizon manipulation by decomposing memory-dependent world-action modeling into memory-grounded planning and plan-conditioned execution, using episodic memory as planning-time evidence to keep executor context fixed.

_spd 6 · gen 3 · spec 8 · cost 6_ · [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11561) · [pdf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.11561v1)

### FARM: Reading Failure Signals from the Internal Predictive States of a Frozen Robotic World Model · **6.69**
Frozen predictive states of a pretrained robotic world model can be directly decoded into failure signals using a tiny readout, achieving strong and transferable failure monitoring with minimal overhead.

_spd 6 · gen 5 · spec 7 · cost 7_ · [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11445) · [pdf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.11445v1) · [code](https://github.com/HaoranPei-casia/FARM)

### CAP: Continuously Adaptive Perception-Blind Humanoid Locomotion via Learned Denoising · **6.45**
CAP is a single-stage humanoid locomotion policy that uses a learned denoising world-model encoder and a co-active proprioceptive variational encoder to maintain robust locomotion even when depth perception is partially corrupted or intermittently unavailable.

_spd – · gen 2 · spec 7 · cost –_ · [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11553) · [pdf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.11553v1)

### UniMPA: A Unified Memory-Prediction-Action Model via Action-Grounded Transition Modeling · **4.49**
UniMPA is a unified memory-prediction-action model that bridges the transition realizability gap in robotic manipulation by using action-grounded transition modeling to resolve ambiguity, assess executability, and adapt actions to current scenes.
