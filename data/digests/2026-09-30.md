## 🏆 New core papers

### Staircase Policy: Streaming Inference for World-Action Models with Large Action Chunks · **7.8**
Staircase Policy introduces a streaming inference and training framework that partitions large action chunks into sub-chunks with staggered denoising, re-predicting future latents from the latest observations to enable fast, long-horizon robotic control.

_spd 8 · gen 6 · spec 8 · cost 6_ · [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36471) · [pdf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.36471v1)

### MVG-WAM: Multiple View Geometry-Aware World-Action Modeling for Robotic Manipulation · **7.46**
MVG-WAM is a multi-view world-action model that explicitly injects geometric structure—via epipolar-constrained global states, view-indexed geometric states, and metric depth supervision—into action prediction, achieving state-of-the-art success on LIBERO, RoboTwin 2.0, and real-robot tasks.

_spd – · gen 7 · spec 8 · cost –_ · [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37793) · [pdf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.37793v1)

### EVO-WAM: Evolving World Action Models through Video-Action Verification · **7.07**
EVO-WAM adapts world action models to unseen robot tasks using self-generated video-action trajectories verified by a vision-language model and inverse dynamics model, boosting success rates substantially without external execution.

### Cooperative Multi-Agent Vision-Language-Action Models via Reinforced Fine Tuning · **6.66**
A three-stage reinforced fine-tuning pipeline for cooperative multi-agent Vision-Language-Action models, combining initialization-aware data collection, offline credit-filtered tuning, and online latent-space fine-tuning, substantially improves success rates across simulation and real-world multi-robot tasks.

### Direct Experience World-Model Optimization: Learning the World Beyond Action Imitation · **6.59**
DEWO is a post-deployment learning paradigm that improves World-Action Models by jointly refining world representations through visual experience and action imitation, boosting control and prediction across simulated and real dexterous manipulation tasks.

### V2X-WAM: A Cooperative World Action Model for End-to-End Autonomous Driving · **6.57**

### CogWAM: Aligning Semantic Cognition with World Action Modeling via Event-Driven Interfaces · **6.54**

### PhysWAM: Physically Consistent World Action Model for Autonomous Driving · **6.53**
PhysWAM is a unified world-action model that couples generated depth and ego motion via a geometric constraint (Coupled Point Projection) to improve planning, depth prediction, and zero-shot transfer in autonomous driving.

### LexiconVLA: Learning Reusable Atomic Action Codebooks for Unseen Tasks · **6.42**
LexiconVLA learns a reusable atomic-action codebook that improves generalization to unseen tasks by decomposing instructions into code-conditioned subtasks, boosting unseen-task success from 16.67% to 34.17% on BridgeVLA.
